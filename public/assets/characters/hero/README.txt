Free Knight — Top-Down Pixel Art Character
==========================================

Free armored knight — Idle, Walk, Sword Attack in 4 directions (80×82)

A free armored Knight with Idle, Walk and a sword-and-shield Attack in four directions, on an 80×82 frame with a shared ground line. Drop him into a top-down RPG prototype today; when he needs something to fight, the Undead Night Horde, Bandit & Mercenary and Korean Folklore Enemies packs share his top-down angle and four directions.
